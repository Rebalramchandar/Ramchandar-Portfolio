PITLA RAMCHANDAR — GEOINFORMATICS

I’m a Geoinformatics postgraduate with a foundation in Computer Science and project experience at National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), ISRO, Hyderabad.

My work brings together GIS, remote sensing, satellite-derived sea surface temperature and Python-based analysis. During my internship, I assessed MODIS Terra Level-2 SST against in-situ buoy Skin SST observations at selected Indian Ocean stations.

I enjoy building reproducible workflows, checking data quality and communicating results through maps, plots and clear technical documentation.

Sea Surface Temperature Assessment over the Coastal Oceans of the Indian Ocean

Assessment of satellite-derived sea surface temperature using MODIS Terra Level-2 data and in-situ buoy Skin SST observations across four selected stations.

  • Python workflows for extraction, quality control, statistics and visualization.

INDIAN OCEAN SCHEMATIC PROJECT VISUAL

Sea Surface Temperature Assessment over the Coastal Oceans of the Indian Ocean

Validation of MODIS Terra Level-2 satellite SST using in-situ buoy Skin SST observations from four selected stations: CB01, CB02, CB06 and CALVAL.

  • Nearest satellite pixel within 5 km of each buoy location.
  • Temporal matchup within a ±60-minute window.
  • Final analysis retained QC0 (BEST) and QC1 (GOOD) observations.
  • Python workflows for extraction, quality control, statistics and visualization.
